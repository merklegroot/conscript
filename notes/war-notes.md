# War notes

## Russia–Ukraine war — simple timeline

### Origins (2014–2021)

- **Feb 2014** — Euromaidan protests; President Viktor Yanukovych flees Ukraine.
- **Mar 2014** — Russia annexes Crimea after a disputed referendum.
- **Apr 2014** — Pro-Russian separatists seize territory in Donetsk and Luhansk; war in Donbas begins.
- **Jul 2014** — Malaysia Airlines Flight MH17 shot down over eastern Ukraine.
- **Feb 2015** — Minsk II ceasefire agreement signed; front lines largely freeze for years.
- **Feb 2022** — Russia formally recognizes the self-declared Donetsk and Luhansk “people’s republics.”

### Full-scale invasion (2022)

- **24 Feb 2022** — Russia launches a full-scale invasion of Ukraine from north, east, and south.
- **Mar 2022** — Russian advance on Kyiv stalls; evidence of atrocities emerges in Bucha and other towns.
- **Apr 2022** — Russia withdraws from northern Ukraine; fighting shifts to the east and south.
- **May 2022** — Last Ukrainian defenders surrender in Mariupol after the siege of Azovstal.
- **Sep 2022** — Ukraine retakes large areas of Kharkiv Oblast in a rapid counteroffensive.
- **Sep 2022** — Russia announces partial mobilization and claims to annex four Ukrainian regions.
- **Oct 2022** — Crimea’s Kerch Bridge is damaged in an explosion.
- **Nov 2022** — Ukraine liberates Kherson city, west of the Dnipro River.

### Stalemate and attrition (2023–2024)

- **Jun 2023** — Ukraine begins a summer counteroffensive; gains are limited against fortified Russian lines.
- **Jun 2023** — Wagner Group leader Yevgeny Prigozhin launches a brief mutiny inside Russia.
- **Feb 2024** — Russian forces capture Avdiivka after months of heavy fighting.
- **Aug 2024** — Ukraine launches a cross-border incursion into Russia’s Kursk Oblast.
- **Nov 2024** — Donald Trump wins the U.S. presidential election, raising expectations of renewed peace diplomacy.

### Diplomacy and continued fighting (2025–present)

- **Jan 2025** — Donald Trump inaugurated; U.S. pushes for a rapid end to the war.
- **Feb 2025** — Tense White House meeting between Trump and President Volodymyr Zelenskyy.
- **May 2025** — Russia and Ukraine hold talks in Istanbul; agree to a large prisoner exchange.
- **2025** — Multiple rounds of U.S.- and Turkish-mediated talks (Istanbul, Abu Dhabi, Geneva, and elsewhere); no lasting ceasefire.
- **Late 2025** — Heavy drone and missile exchanges target cities and energy infrastructure on both sides.
- **Nov 2025** — Ukraine begins counterattacks around Kupyansk in the northeast.
- **Jan–Feb 2026** — Further prisoner exchanges follow talks in Abu Dhabi and Geneva.
- **Early 2026** — Ukraine regains ground in southern Ukraine and western Zaporizhzhia Oblast; Russia continues offensive pressure elsewhere.
- **May 2026** — War enters its fifth year; fighting continues with no negotiated settlement.
