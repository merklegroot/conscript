# Game map

```text
        Home
          |
 Apartment Courtyard
          |
         Town ---- Late-Night Kiosk
          |
    Forest Entry
          |
   Forest Stream
          |
    Deep Forest
```

Main column is the escape route out of the city into the taiga. **Late-Night Kiosk** is a side branch from **Town**. Links are two-way except **Home** (one-way at game start).
