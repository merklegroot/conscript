# Game map

```text
                         Home
                           |
                  Apartment Courtyard
                           |
 Indistrial District --  Town --- Commercial District ---- Convenience Store
                                            |
                                      Forest Entry
                                            |
                                     Forest Stream
                                            |
                                      Deep Forest
```

Main column is the escape route out of the city into the taiga. **Convenience Store** is a side branch from the **Commercial District**. Links are two-way except **Home** (one-way at game start).

## Town districts

The old single **Town** node is split into three connected districts to make the city legible and to give future locations a natural “home”.

### Residential District

- **Role**: housing, courtyards, small parks, local streets
- **Landmarks**: apartment blocks, courtyards, playground/parklet, small clinic/mailboxes
- **Connections**: connects north to **Apartment Courtyard** and south to **Commercial District**
- **Notes**: quiet; more “neighbors”, less foot traffic than commercial

### Commercial District

- **Role**: shops/services, main pedestrian flow, town “center”
- **Landmarks**: convenience store spur, small market stalls, café, pharmacy, bus stop
- **Connections**: between **Residential** (north) and **Industrial** (south); side branch to **Convenience Store**
- **Notes**: best place to hang additional shop/service nodes later (bank, repair, etc.)

### Industrial District

- **Role**: warehouses, rail/yard vibes, utilities, edge-of-town
- **Landmarks**: depot/warehouse row, fenced lots, smokestack/utility building, loading bays
- **Connections**: connects north to **Commercial District** and south to **Forest Entry**
- **Notes**: natural “last stop” before leaving town; good place for barriers, checkpoints, or risky encounters later
